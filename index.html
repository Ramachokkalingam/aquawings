About Aqua Wings

Our Story

Born from a mission to save lives using technology. Aqua Wings emerged from the recognition that traditional water rescue methods often fall short when every second counts. Our innovative approach combines cutting-edge AI, autonomous navigation, and rapid deployment systems to create a solution that acts before it's too late.

Our Mission

To empower every waterbody—whether natural or man-made—with intelligent rescue technology that acts before it's too late.

Our Vision

A world where no one drowns unnoticed. We envision AI drones stationed across beaches, ships, and lakes—ready to respond in seconds.

How It Works

1

Detection

  • Real-time video feed analyzed using a trained AI model
  • Identifies panic gestures, abnormal water motion, or yelling
  • Infrared camera for night detection
2

Navigation

  • Flight controller (Pixhawk) + GPS + SLAM
  • Avoids obstacles autonomously
  • Flight path calculated and adjusted on the fly
3

Rescue Deployment

  • Drone positions itself above victim
  • Lifebuoy or inflatable raft is released precisely
  • Optional: Shouts rescue instructions using speaker
4

Post-Rescue

  • Drone returns to base or hovers nearby
  • Sends rescue confirmation to command center
  • Optional: Sends live footage to emergency services

Technology & Innovation

Drone Frame

Waterproof, lightweight carbon fiber construction

Controller

Pixhawk flight controller for precise navigation

Processor

Raspberry Pi or Jetson Nano for AI processing

Camera

HD + IR support for day and night operations

Deployment System

Servo/motor-controlled payload release mechanism

Battery

LiPo battery system with ~30 minutes runtime

AI Model

Trained on drowning gestures and body language patterns

Navigation

GPS + SLAM + PID control for autonomous flight

Communication

2.4GHz/5GHz RF, 4G LTE fallback connectivity

Failsafe

Obstacle avoidance, auto-return safety features

Data Storage

Footage is processed locally and only essential data is transmitted. No permanent storage of personal imagery.

Anonymized Training

All training data is anonymized and used solely for improving rescue detection accuracy.

No Surveillance Misuse

System is designed exclusively for emergency rescue operations and cannot be used for surveillance purposes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is your AI detection?

Our AI model currently achieves 95% accuracy in controlled testing environments. We continuously improve the model through machine learning and real-world data collection.

What happens in bad weather?

Aqua Wings is designed with weather-resistant materials and has built-in safety protocols. In severe weather, the system automatically grounds itself and alerts operators.

Can it rescue more than one person?

A single drone can carry multiple rescue devices and can be deployed to help multiple victims. Our system also supports coordinated multi-drone operations.

How is it better than lifeguards?

Aqua Wings complements human lifeguards by providing 24/7 monitoring, faster response times, and the ability to reach difficult locations. It's designed to work alongside, not replace, human rescuers.

Is this available commercially?

We're currently in the field testing phase and working towards commercial availability. Contact us to join our pilot program or express interest for future deployment.
